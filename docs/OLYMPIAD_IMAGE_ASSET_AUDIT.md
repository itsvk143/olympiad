---

## 1. Visual Integration Policy
In accordance with Section 8, 9, and 10 of the Master Prompt:
- **No Decorative Images:** Visual assets are integrated only when they enhance conceptual understanding or visual interpretation.
- **Mathematics:** Vector SVG diagrams for geometric proofs, coordinate grids, bar graphs, angles, solid shapes, and pattern reasoning.
- **Science:** Scientific SVG diagrams for circuits, optics, ray diagrams, anatomical systems, plus high-definition authentic photographs for real-world identification (plants, animals, minerals, weather).
- **English:** Visual picture comprehension, story sequencing, and object identification.
